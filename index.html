About the Project

Sustainable Recovery & Resilient Infrastructure

The Bangladesh Sustainable Recovery, Emergency Preparedness and Response Project (B-STRONG) is designed to strengthen sustainable infrastructure, emergency preparedness and resilient development.

Under LGED, digital project monitoring, package-level information, field inspection, implementation progress and reporting can be managed through an integrated Project Management Information System.

  • Package & Scheme Tracking
  • Progress Monitoring
  • Photo & Document Management
